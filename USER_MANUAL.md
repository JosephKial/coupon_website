## Getting Started

### What is the Family Coupon Manager?

The Family Coupon Manager is a private, secure web application that helps your family organize and manage all your coupon codes in one place. Think of it as a digital filing cabinet for all your savings!

### What You Can Do

- ✅ Store all your coupon codes safely
- ✅ Search for coupons quickly
- ✅ Filter coupons by type, value, or expiration date
- ✅ Track which coupons you've used
- ✅ See how much money you're saving
- ✅ Access your coupons from any device
- ✅ Share coupons with family members

### Accessing the Application

1. **Open your web browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
2. **Go to your family's coupon website** (your family admin will provide this)
3. **Bookmark the page** for easy access later

## Creating Your Account

### First Time Setup

1. **Click "Register"** on the login page
2. **Fill in your information**:
   - **Email**: Use your personal email address
   - **Password**: Create a strong password (see tips below)
   - **First Name**: Your first name
   - **Last Name**: Your last name
3. **Click "Create Account"**
4. **You're ready to start!**

### Password Tips 🔐

Your password should be:
- At least 8 characters long
- Include uppercase letters (A-Z)
- Include lowercase letters (a-z)
- Include numbers (0-9)
- Include special characters (@, $, !, %, *, ?, &)

**Good example**: `MyFamily2024!`
**Bad example**: `password123`

### Logging In

1. **Enter your email address**
2. **Enter your password**
3. **Click "Sign In"**

**Tip**: Check "Remember me" if you're using your personal device.

## Managing Your Coupons

### Adding a New Coupon

1. **Click the "Add Coupon" button** (usually a + icon)
2. **Fill in the coupon details**:

   **Required Information**:
   - **Coupon Code**: The actual code (e.g., "SAVE20", "FREESHIP")
   - **Discount Type**: Choose "Amount" (like $5 off) or "Percentage" (like 20% off)
   - **Value**: The discount amount (e.g., 5.00 for $5 off, or 20 for 20% off)

   **Optional Information**:
   - **Description**: What the coupon is for (e.g., "20% off all items")
   - **Expiration Date**: When the coupon expires
   - **Usage Limit**: How many times it can be used
   - **Tags**: Categories like "grocery", "restaurant", "clothing"

3. **Click "Save Coupon"**

### Editing a Coupon

1. **Find the coupon** you want to edit
2. **Click the "Edit" button** (pencil icon)
3. **Make your changes**
4. **Click "Save Changes"**

### Deleting a Coupon

1. **Find the coupon** you want to delete
2. **Click the "Delete" button** (trash icon)
3. **Confirm deletion** when prompted

**⚠️ Warning**: Deleted coupons cannot be recovered!

### Marking a Coupon as Used

1. **Find the coupon** you used
2. **Click "Mark as Used"** or change the status to "Used"
3. **The coupon will be marked** but kept for your records

## Finding Coupons

### Basic Search

1. **Use the search box** at the top of the page
2. **Type part of the coupon code** or description
3. **Results appear automatically** as you type

**Example**: Type "SAVE" to find all coupons with "SAVE" in the code or description.

### Advanced Filtering

Use the filter options to narrow down your search:

#### By Status
- **Active**: Coupons you can still use
- **Expired**: Coupons that have passed their expiration date
- **Used**: Coupons you've already used
- **Disabled**: Coupons you've temporarily disabled

#### By Discount Type
- **Amount**: Fixed dollar amounts (like $5 off)
- **Percentage**: Percentage discounts (like 20% off)

#### By Value Range
- Set minimum and maximum discount values
- **Example**: Find all coupons worth between $10 and $50

#### By Expiration Date
- **Expiring Soon**: Find coupons that expire within a certain timeframe
- **Date Range**: Find coupons expiring between specific dates

#### By Tags
- Filter by categories you've created
- **Examples**: "grocery", "restaurant", "online", "clothing"

### Sorting Options

Sort your coupons by:
- **Newest First**: Recently added coupons
- **Oldest First**: Coupons added longest ago
- **Expiration Date**: Soonest to expire first
- **Highest Value**: Most valuable coupons first
- **Alphabetical**: By coupon code

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### "I can't log in"
- **Check your email and password** for typos
- **Make sure Caps Lock is off**
- **Try resetting your password** if you've forgotten it
- **Contact your family admin** if you're still having trouble

#### "The website won't load"
- **Check your internet connection**
- **Try refreshing the page** (F5 or Ctrl+R)
- **Clear your browser cache** and try again
- **Try a different browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)

#### "I can't find my coupon"
- **Check your spelling** in the search box
- **Try searching for part of the code** instead of the whole thing
- **Check if it's filtered out** by your current filter settings
- **Look in different status categories** (Active, Used, Expired)

#### "The coupon won't save"
- **Make sure all required fields are filled in**
- **Check that the coupon code doesn't already exist**
- **Verify the expiration date is in the future**
- **Try refreshing the page** and entering it again

#### "I accidentally deleted a coupon"
- **Contact your family admin** - they may be able to restore it from a backup
- **Re-enter the coupon** if you remember the details

### Getting Help

If you're still having trouble:

1. **Ask a family member** who's familiar with the system
2. **Contact your family admin** - they have access to more detailed help
3. **Take a screenshot** of any error messages to help with troubleshooting

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Can I access this from anywhere?**
A: Yes! You can access your coupons from any device with internet access and a web browser.

**Q: Is my information secure?**
A: Yes! The system uses bank-level security to protect your information. Only your family members can see the coupons.

**Q: Can I use this on my phone?**
A: Absolutely! The website works great on phones and tablets.

**Q: How many coupons can I store?**
A: There's no limit! Store as many coupons as you need.

### Coupon Management

**Q: What if I don't know the expiration date?**
A: That's okay! You can leave the expiration date blank and add it later if you find out.

**Q: Can I edit a coupon after I save it?**
A: Yes! You can edit any coupon you've created at any time.

**Q: What happens to expired coupons?**
A: They stay in the system but are marked as expired. You can still see them for reference.

**Q: Can other family members see my coupons?**
A: Yes, all family members can see and use all coupons in the system. It's designed for sharing!

### Technical Questions

**Q: Do I need to install anything?**
A: No! It works in your web browser - no downloads or installations needed.

**Q: What browsers work best?**
A: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge all work great. Make sure your browser is up to date.

**Q: Can I use this offline?**
A: You need internet to add or edit coupons, but recently viewed coupons may be available offline.

**Q: What if I forget my password?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page, or contact your family admin.

### Usage Tips

**Q: How should I organize my coupons?**
A: Use tags! Create categories like "grocery", "restaurant", "online", or specific store names.

**Q: Should I delete used coupons?**
A: No! Mark them as "Used" instead. This helps track your savings and keeps a record.

**Q: How can I find coupons that expire soon?**
A: Use the expiration date filter to find coupons expiring in the next week or month.

**Q: Can I print coupons from this system?**
A: The system stores coupon codes, not printable coupons. You'll need to go to the retailer's website to print physical coupons.
